Fill this in for your machine. RULES.md tells the agent to read facts here instead of guessing them. Keep only facts
that change rarely; live facts (ports, containers, hostnames) go in `~/.claude/FLEET.md`, generated by
`~/.claude/scripts/fleet-facts.py`.

## Agent-brain repos
